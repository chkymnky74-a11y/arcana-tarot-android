How to Use Arcana Tarot

What this app does

Arcana Tarot helps tarot readers build smoother readings with the Fate & Feather deck. Ask a question, choose a spread, pick or draw the cards, choose a reading style, and use the generated script as a guide while reading for someone.

Simple steps

1. Tap Start a New Reading.

2. Type the question, then tap Next.

3. Choose a spread, then tap Next.

4. Select the cards pulled and whether each is upright or reversed, or tap Draw the Cards for Me. Then tap Next.

5. Choose a reading style and tap Create Reading Script.

Use Back at any step to change something. Nothing you entered is lost when you go back.

Your readings are saved automatically

Every reading is saved to Saved Readings the moment you create it. A clarifier card is added to the same saved reading. Starting a new reading never erases an old one.

Continue Reading

If you close the app partway through, your question, spread, and cards are kept. Tap Continue Reading on the opening screen to pick up exactly where you stopped.

Saved Readings

Open Saved Readings from the opening screen to see every reading, newest first. Tap one to read it again, copy it, open it to add a clarifier, or delete it.

Real deck style

When a card is chosen in a spread, it is removed from the other card lists so the same card cannot repeat in the same reading. This helps the app feel more like working with a real tarot deck.

What does reversed mean?

A reversed card does not always mean something bad. It can mean blocked energy, delays, inner work, avoidance, imbalance, hesitation, or the shadow side of the upright meaning.

What each section means

The Message of Your Reading: A personal interpretation that connects all the cards into one message for your question, including what the clarifier adds.

Quick Message: A short overview of the reading.

Card Breakdown: A quick guide showing the card position, card pulled, upright or reversed direction, and its Fate & Feather meaning.

Say This: A complete spoken reading, written to be read aloud to the person, card by card and in your chosen reading style. When you pull a clarifier, it is woven into the conversation.

Follow-Up Question: A natural question to keep the reading flowing or offer another card pull.

Pull Clarifier Card

Use this when the person wants one more card for clarity, why the answer is showing up, their feelings, their next move, or the best next step. The clarifier comes from the cards not already in the spread.

Copy Reading Script

Use this when you want to paste the reading into chat, notes, messages, documents, or a client reply.

New Reading

This clears the screen so you can start fresh. The reading you just finished stays in Saved Readings.

Do I read everything out loud?

No. The Say This section is the main part to speak from. The rest helps you understand the spread quickly.

Can I change the wording?

Yes. The script is a guide, not a cage. Add your own intuition, tone, and natural wording so it sounds like you.
